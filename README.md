# Pantheon

<sub>The relationship OS that runs itself</sub>

<br>

Your chats become a CRM that updates itself, across **Telegram**, **WhatsApp** and **iMessage**, plus your **X** contacts.<br>
Contacts, companies and deals stay current from the conversations you're already having.

## What Pantheon does

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**One inbox for every chat**<br>
<sub>Telegram, WhatsApp and iMessage side by side, with each person's history kept together across channels.</sub>

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**A CRM that updates itself**<br>
<sub>Conversations build and update your contacts, companies and deals, so nobody keeps the CRM by hand.</sub>

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Milo, your assistant**<br>
<sub>Drafts replies, remembers context and turns everyday conversations into structured relationship data.</sub>

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Works with your AI tools**<br>
<sub>Connect Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and other AI tools to your Pantheon account.</sub>

</td>
</tr>
</table>

## Get Pantheon

| | |
|---|---|
| **Web** | [app.pantheon.run](https://app.pantheon.run) |
| **Mac** | [pantheon.run/download](https://pantheon.run/download) — Telegram, WhatsApp and iMessage in one inbox on your Mac |
| **AI tools** | Paste `https://mcp.pantheon.run/mcp` into Claude or ChatGPT → Settings → Connectors. [Guide](https://docs.pantheon.run/guides/connect-ai-agent) |
| **Command line** | `curl -fsSL https://pantheon.run/install.sh \| sh` (macOS / Linux), `irm https://pantheon.run/install.ps1 \| iex` (Windows), or `npm install -g runpantheon` |

## Get help

- **Something broken?** [Open a bug report](https://github.com/runpantheon/pantheon/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- **Want something?** [Request a feature](https://github.com/runpantheon/pantheon/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) or [ask for a channel](https://github.com/runpantheon/pantheon/issues/new?template=channel_request.yml).
- **A question, or an idea to talk through?** [Start a discussion](https://github.com/runpantheon/pantheon/discussions).
- **Your account, billing or anything private?** Email [support@pantheon.run](mailto:support@pantheon.run). Please don't post personal details or message content in public issues.
- **From the command line:** `pantheon feedback "<what happened>"` sends a report straight to us.
